What we build

We focus on tools that people actually reach for every day — writing helpers, SEO utilities, social media tools, career tools, and developer utilities. If you find yourself Googling "free online [tool]" regularly, we're probably building it.

How we stay free

Ignitool is supported by advertising through Google AdSense. We keep ads minimal and non-intrusive — one or two placements per page, never pop-ups, never interstitials. The tools come first.
