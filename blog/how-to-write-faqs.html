How to Write FAQs for Your Website That Actually Help

By the Ignitool team July 19, 2026 6 min read

A well-written FAQ section does three things at once: it reduces customer support volume, increases conversion rates by addressing objections before they become reasons not to buy, and improves SEO by capturing question-based search queries. Most FAQ sections do none of these things — because they answer the wrong questions in the wrong way.

Why FAQ sections matter more than most people think

📉
Reduce support costs
Every FAQ that answers a common question is one fewer support ticket, email, or chat. At scale, this is significant.
💰
Increase conversions
Unanswered questions become reasons not to buy. FAQs address objections before they block the purchase decision.
🔍
Earn Google rich snippets
FAQ schema markup can display your Q&As directly in search results — doubling your visible real estate without changing your ranking.
🏆
Win Featured Snippets
Question-based FAQs often win Google's Featured Snippet position — appearing above all organic results for specific queries.

The most common FAQ mistake — answering the wrong questions

Most FAQ sections are written from the company's perspective — questions the marketing team thinks customers should ask, or questions that showcase product features. The best FAQ sections are written from the customer's perspective — questions buyers actually have before, during, and after the purchase decision.

The three best sources for real customer questions are:

  1. Your support inbox — questions that appear more than once belong in your FAQ
  2. Google's "People also ask" section — search your product or service category and collect every question Google surfaces
  3. Reviews and Q&A sections on Amazon, Google My Business, Trustpilot, or your own site — real customers asking real questions in their own words

How to write FAQ answers that convert

The question is only half the FAQ. The answer determines whether the visitor feels confident enough to proceed.

Keep answers concise and direct

Answer the question in the first sentence. Don't build up to the answer — give it immediately, then provide supporting detail if necessary. People reading FAQs are looking for quick reassurance, not a full explanation.

❌ Buries the answer

Q: How long does shipping take?
We work with several shipping partners and the delivery time can vary depending on a number of factors including your location, the shipping method you select at checkout, and current carrier processing times. Generally speaking, most orders...

✅ Leads with the answer

Q: How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (2 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee. Orders placed before 2pm EST ship the same day.

Use the customer's language

Write FAQ questions the way your customers ask them — not the way your legal or marketing team would phrase them. "Can I get a refund?" converts better than "What is your returns policy?" because it's how people actually think.

Address the underlying concern, not just the surface question

When someone asks "Is this safe?" they're really asking "Will this hurt me or waste my money?" When someone asks "How long does it take to set up?" they're really asking "Is this going to be complicated and frustrating?" Answer both the question and the underlying concern.

FAQ schema — how to get rich snippets in Google

FAQ schema is structured data markup that tells Google your page contains a FAQ section. When Google recognizes it, your questions and answers can appear as expandable snippets directly in search results — dramatically increasing your search visibility without changing your ranking.

▾ What is FAQ schema markup?
FAQ schema is structured data that tells Google your page contains FAQs. When detected, Google can display your Q&As directly in search results as expandable snippets.
▸ How many FAQs do I need for rich snippets?

To add FAQ schema to your page, you need to include a JSON-LD script in your page's HTML head section. The format looks like this:

FAQ JSON-LD Schema Format

{"@context": "https://schema.org", "@type": "FAQPage", "mainEntity": [{"@type": "Question", "name": "Your question here?", "acceptedAnswer": {"@type": "Answer", "text": "Your answer here."}}]}

You need at least 2 questions for FAQ rich snippet eligibility. Google recommends ensuring every question has a complete, accurate answer before submitting for rich results.

How many FAQs should you have?

  • Product pages: 5-8 questions covering the most common pre-purchase objections and concerns
  • Service pages: 8-12 questions covering process, pricing, timeline, and results
  • Dedicated FAQ page: 15-30 questions organized by category
  • Blog posts: 4-6 questions at the end covering gaps the article didn't address

Questions every product or service FAQ should answer

  • How does it work / what do I get?
  • How much does it cost / what's included?
  • How long does it take to see results / get delivery?
  • What if I'm not satisfied / what's your return policy?
  • Is it right for me / who is this for?
  • How is this different from [main competitor]?
  • What do I need to get started?
  • How do I contact you if I have a problem?
