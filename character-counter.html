Frequently asked questions

What does a character counter do? +
A character counter counts every individual character in your text — including letters, numbers, punctuation, and spaces. It's essential for social media posts, SMS messages, meta descriptions, and any platform with a strict character limit.
How many characters does Twitter allow? +
Twitter (now called X) allows a maximum of 280 characters per tweet for standard accounts. URLs are always counted as 23 characters regardless of their actual length. Twitter Blue / X Premium subscribers may have access to longer posts.
What is the character limit for LinkedIn posts? +
LinkedIn posts have a 3,000 character limit. After about 210 characters, LinkedIn adds a "see more" cut-off. For best engagement, aim to put your most important content in the first 200 characters so it appears above the fold in the feed.
How many characters can an SMS message have? +
A single SMS message is limited to 160 characters. If your message exceeds 160 characters, it is split into multiple messages (each billed separately). Messages over 160 characters are typically concatenated into a single MMS or multi-part SMS depending on the carrier.
What is the ideal meta description length? +
Google typically displays meta descriptions up to 160 characters. Descriptions longer than 160 characters may be truncated in search results. For best results, write meta descriptions between 120 and 155 characters — long enough to be descriptive, short enough to avoid truncation.
