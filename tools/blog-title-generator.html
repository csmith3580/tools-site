Frequently asked questions

What makes a good SEO blog title? +
A great SEO blog title includes the target keyword near the beginning, is between 50–60 characters so it doesn't get cut off in search results, creates curiosity or communicates clear value, and matches what the reader is searching for. Numbers, power words, and questions consistently outperform generic titles.
How long should a blog post title be? +
Keep blog titles between 50–60 characters for SEO. Google displays roughly 60 characters in search results — anything longer gets truncated with "..." which reduces click-through rates. Our generator aims for titles in this range for maximum search visibility.
Is this blog title generator free? +
Yes, completely free with no sign-up required. Generate titles for as many blog posts as you need. Ignitool is supported by advertising, which keeps all tools free for everyone.
What types of blog titles get the most clicks? +
Studies consistently show that listicles ("10 Ways to..."), how-to titles ("How to X in Y Steps"), and question-based titles generate the highest click-through rates. Numbers in titles boost CTR by up to 36%. Our generator produces a mix of all these proven formats.
Can I use these titles for YouTube videos too? +
Yes — many of the title formats generated work equally well for YouTube videos. For YouTube-specific title optimization, also try our YouTube Title Generator tool, which is tuned specifically for video content and YouTube's search algorithm.
