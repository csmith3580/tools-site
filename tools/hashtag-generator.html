Frequently asked questions

How many hashtags should I use on Instagram? +
Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags per post. Research suggests 3–5 highly relevant hashtags perform best for reach in 2026, as Instagram's algorithm now prioritizes relevance over quantity. Our generator balances popular, mid-range, and niche hashtags to maximize your reach.
Are these hashtags free to use? +
Yes, completely. All hashtags generated are free to copy and use on any platform. No sign-up, no watermarks, no limits on how many times you use the tool.
Why are hashtags different for each platform? +
Each platform has different hashtag culture. Instagram hashtags are discovery-focused. TikTok hashtags trend faster and use more casual language. LinkedIn hashtags are professional and industry-specific. Twitter hashtags are often tied to news and real-time conversations. Our AI tailors the output to each platform's norms.
How many hashtags work best on TikTok? +
TikTok recommends using 3–5 hashtags per video. Using too many can look spammy and reduce reach. Always include at least one trending hashtag, one niche hashtag, and one broad category hashtag for the best balance of discoverability.
Can I use the same hashtags on every post? +
No — using identical hashtags on every post can trigger Instagram's spam filters and reduce your reach. Generate fresh hashtag sets for each post and rotate between them. Our tool makes this easy by generating a new set every time.
