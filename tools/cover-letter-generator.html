Frequently asked questions

How long should a cover letter be? +
A cover letter should be between 250–400 words — roughly three to four paragraphs. It should fit on a single page. Hiring managers spend an average of 7 seconds scanning a cover letter before deciding whether to read it, so concise and impactful is always better than long and detailed.
Do cover letters still matter in 2026? +
Yes. While some companies don't require them, a well-written cover letter significantly increases your chances when it is requested. Studies show that 49% of hiring managers consider a cover letter a critical factor in their decision. When applying to competitive roles, a tailored cover letter can differentiate you from equally-qualified candidates.
Is this cover letter generator free? +
Yes, completely free with no sign-up required. Generate as many cover letters as you need for different job applications. Each generation creates a unique letter tailored to the specific job and company you enter.
How do I customize the generated cover letter? +
After generating, copy the text and paste it into Google Docs or Word. Then personalize it further by adding specific company details, referencing the hiring manager's name if you know it, and adjusting any phrases to better match your natural voice. The AI gives you a strong foundation — your personal touches make it stand out.
Can I generate multiple versions for the same job? +
Yes. Click "Regenerate" to create a different version with the same information. Each generation uses a different structure and phrasing. You can generate several versions and choose the one that sounds most like you, or combine the best parts of different versions.
