Frequently asked questions

What is an AI text summarizer? +
An AI text summarizer is a tool that uses artificial intelligence to read a piece of text and condense it into a shorter version that captures the main points. Instead of reading a 2,000-word article, you can get the key ideas in 5 bullet points in seconds. Our summarizer uses a large language model to understand context and meaning, not just extract sentences.
How long can the text be? +
There is no strict character limit, but for best results paste text between 200 and 10,000 words. Very short texts (under 100 words) may not have enough content to summarize meaningfully. Very long texts (over 15,000 words) may be truncated by the AI model's context window.
Is this AI text summarizer free? +
Yes, completely free. No account, no sign-up, and no daily usage limits. Ignitool is supported by advertising, which keeps all tools free for everyone.
What types of text can I summarize? +
You can summarize almost any type of text — news articles, academic papers, blog posts, emails, meeting notes, legal documents, book chapters, research reports, and more. The AI adapts its summary style to the type of content it detects.
Is my text stored or saved? +
No. Your text is sent to the AI model only to generate the summary and is not stored on our servers. We do not log, save, or use your text for any other purpose. Once you close the page, your input is gone.
