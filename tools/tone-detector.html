Frequently asked questions

What is a tone detector? +
A tone detector analyzes written text and identifies its emotional quality — whether it sounds professional, friendly, aggressive, formal, casual, or something else. AI tone detectors go beyond keyword matching to understand the overall feel and intent of writing, helping you ensure your message lands the way you intend.
Why does tone matter in writing? +
Tone is what your reader feels when they read your writing — even when you don't intend a particular tone. An email intended to be direct can read as rude. A message meant to be casual can seem unprofessional. Checking tone before sending important communications helps you avoid misunderstandings, especially in written contexts where body language and vocal inflection are absent.
What tones can this detector identify? +
Our AI tone detector identifies a wide range of tones including: professional, formal, casual, friendly, enthusiastic, confident, aggressive, passive-aggressive, sarcastic, empathetic, neutral, and more. It also rates your text across specific dimensions like formality, positivity, assertiveness, and clarity.
