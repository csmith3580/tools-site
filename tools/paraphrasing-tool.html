Frequently asked questions

What is a paraphrasing tool? +
A paraphrasing tool rewrites text while preserving the original meaning. Unlike copying text, paraphrasing changes the wording, sentence structure, and phrasing. It's used by students to avoid plagiarism, writers to improve clarity, and professionals to adapt content for different audiences.
How is AI paraphrasing different from manual rewriting? +
AI paraphrasing goes beyond simple word substitution. The AI understands the meaning and context of the original text and produces a genuinely rewritten version — adjusting sentence structure, vocabulary, and flow while keeping the core meaning intact. This produces more natural results than tools that only swap synonyms.
Is this paraphrasing tool free? +

What tone options are available? +
