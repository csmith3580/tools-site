Frequently asked questions

How long should an Instagram caption be? +
Instagram allows captions up to 2,200 characters, but only the first 125 characters show before the "more" cutoff in the feed. Put your hook or most important message in the first line. Research shows captions between 138–150 characters get the best engagement for most niches.
Should captions be different for each platform? +
Yes. LinkedIn captions should be professional and insightful. Instagram captions can be casual with emojis and line breaks. Twitter captions must be concise and punchy. TikTok captions are short and trend-aware. Our generator automatically adapts the style for whichever platform you select.
Is this social media caption generator free? +
Yes, completely free with no sign-up required. Generate captions for as many posts as you need across all platforms. Ignitool is supported by advertising to keep all tools free.
Should I include a call to action in my captions? +
Yes — posts with a clear call to action (CTA) get significantly higher engagement. Good CTAs include "Save this for later," "Tag a friend who needs this," "Drop your answer below," and "Link in bio." Our generator includes platform-appropriate CTAs in most captions automatically.
Can I use these captions for client social media accounts? +
Yes. All content generated by Ignitool is yours to use for personal or commercial purposes, including client work. We recommend reviewing and personalizing each caption to match the client's unique voice before posting.
