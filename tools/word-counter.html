Frequently asked questions

What is a word counter? +
A word counter is a tool that counts the number of words in a block of text. It also typically counts characters, sentences, paragraphs, and estimates reading time. Writers, students, bloggers, and social media managers use word counters to meet platform limits or writing goals.
Is this word counter free? +
Yes, completely free. No sign-up, no account, and no limits on how much text you can count. All processing happens in your browser — your text is never sent to any server.
How does the reading time estimate work? +
Reading time is calculated based on the average adult reading speed of 238 words per minute, which is the figure used by most studies on reading comprehension. A 500-word article takes approximately 2 minutes to read.
Does this tool work for essays and academic writing? +
Yes. The word counter works for any type of writing — essays, articles, blog posts, social media captions, emails, and more. The writing goals section specifically shows progress toward common academic word counts like college essays (650 words).
What is the difference between characters with and without spaces? +
Characters with spaces counts every single character including spaces. Characters without spaces counts only letters, numbers, and punctuation — excluding spaces. Some platforms like Twitter count characters including spaces; others count only non-space characters.
