Why Product Descriptions Matter for eCommerce SEO

Product descriptions serve two equally important purposes in ecommerce: they help search engines understand what your product is so they can rank it for relevant searches, and they persuade human visitors to make a purchase. Thin or duplicate product descriptions are one of the most common SEO issues for ecommerce sites — Google penalizes pages with low-quality or copied content, pushing them down in search rankings. Unique, detailed product descriptions that naturally include relevant keywords help products rank higher in both Google and marketplace search algorithms. Studies show that 87% of online shoppers say detailed product descriptions are very important to their purchase decision.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a good product description? +
A great product description focuses on benefits, not just features. Instead of "60-hour burn time," say "Enjoy 60 hours of calm, consistent fragrance." It speaks directly to the target customer, uses sensory language where appropriate, addresses common objections, and ends with a subtle call to action.
Which length should I use for my product? +
Short descriptions (50 words) work best for product listing pages where shoppers browse many items. Medium descriptions (100 words) are ideal for Etsy and Shopify product pages. Long descriptions (200 words) are best for Amazon where keyword-rich copy helps SEO and conversion, and for high-ticket items that need more persuasion.
Is this product description generator free? +
Yes, completely free with no sign-up required. Generate descriptions for as many products as you need. Ignitool is supported by advertising to keep all tools free for everyone.
Can I use these descriptions for Amazon listings? +
Yes. The long description format is optimized for Amazon's A+ content and standard product descriptions. For best results on Amazon, also add relevant keywords naturally throughout the description. Consider using our long version as your main product description and your short version as the Amazon bullet points starting point.
How do I make the description unique to my brand? +
After generating, personalize by adding your brand name, story, or unique selling proposition. Add any specific certifications, materials, or origin details. Adjust the tone to match your brand voice. The AI gives you a strong structural foundation — your unique brand details make it convert better.
