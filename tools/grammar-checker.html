Frequently asked questions

What does this grammar checker fix? +
Our AI grammar checker identifies and fixes grammar errors (subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, misplaced modifiers), spelling mistakes, punctuation errors (comma splices, missing apostrophes), and style issues (wordiness, passive voice, unclear phrasing). It explains each error so you understand what went wrong.
Is this better than basic spell check? +
Yes, significantly. Basic spell checkers only flag misspelled words. Our AI grammar checker understands context — it catches correctly spelled words used incorrectly (like "their" vs "there"), complex grammar errors, and style improvements that no spell checker can detect.
Is this grammar checker free? +
Yes, completely free with no sign-up required. Unlike Grammarly which requires a paid subscription for advanced checks, Ignitool's grammar checker is free for everyone with no usage limits.
What types of writing does it work for? +
It works for all types of writing — emails, essays, blog posts, business reports, academic papers, social media posts, cover letters, and more. The AI adapts its suggestions based on the context and formality of your writing.
Is my text kept private? +
Yes. Your text is sent only to the AI to generate corrections and is not stored on our servers. We do not save, log, or use your text for training purposes. Once you close or refresh the page, your input is gone.
