Frequently asked questions

What is a FAQ section and why is it important? +
A FAQ section answers common questions visitors have before purchasing or taking action. Well-written FAQs reduce customer support volume, increase conversion rates by addressing objections, and improve SEO by targeting question-based search queries. Google also displays FAQ rich snippets in search results, which can dramatically increase click-through rates.
What is JSON-LD FAQ schema? +
JSON-LD FAQ schema is structured data markup that tells Google your page contains a FAQ section. When Google recognizes this, it can show your questions and answers directly in search results as expandable rich snippets — giving you more visibility without users having to click. Our generator produces the correct schema format automatically.
How many FAQ questions should I have? +
For a product page or landing page, 5–8 questions covering the most common objections and queries is ideal. For a dedicated FAQ page or help center, 10–20 is typical. For SEO content targeting question-based keywords, 10+ questions with detailed answers tends to rank best. Google recommends at least 2 questions for FAQ rich results eligibility.
Is this FAQ generator free? +
Yes, completely free with no sign-up required. Generate FAQs for as many products or topics as you need. The JSON-LD schema is also included free. Ignitool is supported by advertising to keep all tools free for everyone.
How do I add the FAQ schema to my website? +
Copy the JSON-LD schema code generated by this tool, then paste it inside the <head> section of your webpage's HTML. In WordPress, you can use a plugin like Yoast SEO or RankMath to add schema without touching code. In Shopify, add the script to your theme's theme.liquid file. After adding, test it using Google's Rich Results Test tool.
