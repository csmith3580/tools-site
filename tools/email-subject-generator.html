Frequently asked questions

What makes a good email subject line? +
The best email subject lines are specific, create curiosity or urgency, and are under 50 characters so they don't get cut off on mobile. They avoid spam trigger words like "FREE!!!" in all caps, use personalization when possible, and clearly hint at the value inside the email.
How long should an email subject line be? +
Keep subject lines under 50 characters for mobile devices, where over 60% of emails are opened. The sweet spot is 30–50 characters. Our generator shows the character count for each subject line so you can pick the best one for your audience.
Is this email subject line generator free? +
Yes, completely free. No sign-up, no daily limits, no watermarks. Generate as many subject lines as you need for newsletters, marketing campaigns, cold outreach, and transactional emails.
Should I use emojis in subject lines? +
Emojis can increase open rates by 25–30% when used sparingly and appropriately. One emoji at the start or end of a subject line works well for B2C audiences. For B2B or professional emails, stick to text-only subject lines to maintain credibility.
What words should I avoid in subject lines? +
Avoid spam trigger words like "free money," "click here," "guaranteed," "no risk," excessive exclamation marks, and all-caps words. These can send your email to the spam folder. Our AI is trained to avoid these patterns automatically.
