Frequently asked questions

How long should a LinkedIn About section be? +
LinkedIn allows up to 2,600 characters in the About section, but only the first 300 characters show before the "see more" cutoff. Put your most compelling hook in the first 2–3 lines. The ideal length is 300–600 characters for maximum readability, though thought leaders and executives often write longer, story-driven bios of 1,000+ characters.
Should I write my LinkedIn bio in first or third person? +
First person ("I help companies...") is recommended for most professionals. It feels more human and direct. Third person ("Alex is a product manager who...") is occasionally used by executives and public figures but can feel cold and impersonal in a social network context. Our generator defaults to first person.
What should I include in my LinkedIn About section? +
A strong LinkedIn About section includes: who you are and what you do, who you help or what problems you solve, your key achievements with specific numbers, your unique skills or approach, and a call to action (connect with me, message me, visit my website). Our generator covers all five elements.
How often should I update my LinkedIn bio? +
Update your LinkedIn About section whenever you change roles, gain significant new achievements, shift career direction, or are actively job searching. A fresh bio also signals activity to the LinkedIn algorithm, which can increase your profile's visibility in search results.
