Frequently asked questions

What makes a good cold email? +
The best cold emails are short (under 150 words), highly personalized, lead with value not features, have one clear call to action, and feel human rather than templated. Avoid attachments in first emails, use simple formatting, and always end with a low-friction ask like a yes/no question rather than scheduling a long meeting.
How long should a cold email be? +
Under 150 words is ideal. Studies show cold emails under 125 words get higher reply rates than longer ones. Decision-makers scan their inbox — you have 3–4 seconds to capture attention. Our generator targets this range across all three variations.
What are the 3 cold email approaches? +
Problem-led emails open by identifying a pain point the prospect likely has — then position your solution. Compliment-led emails open with genuine praise about the company or prospect before making the ask. Direct ask emails skip the setup and get straight to the point — these work best with senior decision-makers who receive many emails daily.
Is this cold email generator free? +
Yes, completely free with no sign-up required. Generate cold emails for as many prospects as you need. Ignitool is supported by advertising to keep all tools free.
What is a good cold email reply rate? +
A good cold email reply rate is 5–10%. The average is 1–5%. If you are seeing under 1%, your targeting or personalization needs work. The biggest factors are: sending to the right person, personalizing the first line, keeping it short, and having a relevant value proposition.
