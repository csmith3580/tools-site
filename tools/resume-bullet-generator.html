Frequently asked questions

What makes a strong resume bullet point? +
Strong resume bullets follow the CAR formula: Challenge, Action, Result. Start with a powerful action verb (led, drove, built, grew), describe what you did, and quantify the result with a specific number or percentage. Avoid passive language like "was responsible for" — use active verbs that show ownership and impact.
What are the best action verbs for resume bullets? +
The best resume action verbs depend on your field. For leadership roles: led, directed, spearheaded, championed. For growth: grew, scaled, expanded, increased. For improvement: optimized, streamlined, reduced, improved. For creation: built, developed, designed, launched. Our generator automatically selects the most impactful verbs for your specific role.
What is ATS and why does it matter? +
ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System — software used by 75% of employers to screen resumes before a human reads them. ATS systems scan for keywords, action verbs, and relevant skills. Resumes not optimized for ATS often get filtered out before reaching a hiring manager. Our generator creates bullets that balance ATS optimization with human readability.
How many bullet points should each job have? +
For your most recent and relevant roles, use 4–6 bullet points. For older or less relevant roles, use 2–3. Focus on impact and results rather than listing every task. Recruiters spend an average of 7 seconds scanning a resume — quality over quantity is essential.
