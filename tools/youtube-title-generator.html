Frequently asked questions

What makes a good YouTube title? +
A great YouTube title is specific, creates curiosity or communicates clear value, includes a searchable keyword, and is under 70 characters. Titles with numbers, power words like "secret," "proven," or "mistake," and emotional triggers consistently outperform generic titles. The first 60 characters show before truncation on mobile.
How long should a YouTube title be? +
YouTube allows up to 100 characters in titles, but only the first 60–70 characters show in search results and on mobile. Keep your most important keywords and hook in the first 60 characters. Our generator shows the character count for each title so you can choose the best one.
Do YouTube titles affect SEO? +
Yes, significantly. YouTube is the world's second largest search engine. Your title is the most important on-page SEO factor for YouTube. Including your target keyword naturally near the beginning of the title helps YouTube understand what your video is about and rank it for relevant searches.
Should I use clickbait titles? +
Misleading clickbait hurts your channel long-term by increasing bounce rate when viewers feel deceived. However, compelling, curiosity-driven titles that accurately reflect your content are highly effective. The key difference: your title should create curiosity that your video genuinely delivers on.
