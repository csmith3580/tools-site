Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolsAI ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and tools at this domain.

Information we collect

We collect minimal information to operate and improve our service:

  • Usage data: We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about how visitors use our site, including pages visited, time spent, and browser type. This data does not identify you personally.
  • Tool input: Text you enter into our free tools (word counter, case converter, AI tools, etc.) is processed to generate results. For pure JavaScript tools (word counter, character counter, case converter), your text never leaves your browser. For AI-powered tools, your text is sent to the AI API provider to generate a result and is not stored.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for Google Analytics and Google AdSense. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How we use your information

  • To provide and improve our free tools
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • To analyze site usage and improve user experience

Google AdSense and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out through the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: Website analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense: Advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic API: Powers AI-based tools. Text inputs are sent to Anthropic for processing. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Data retention

We do not store the text you enter into our tools. Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics per their standard retention settings.

Children's privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page with an updated revision date.
